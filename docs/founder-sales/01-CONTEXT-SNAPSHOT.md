# Comply Vault — Founder Sales Sprint Pack

## 1) Context Snapshot

**Product (1 sentence):**
Comply Vault turns RIA meeting recordings/notes into SEC audit-ready supervision documentation (transcripts + evidence-linked summaries + flags + export packs) in minutes — replacing manual note-taking, Word/Google Docs templates, and spreadsheet-based checklists.

**ICP wedge (who + where):**
Chief Compliance Officer / ops owner at small SEC-registered RIAs (1–20 advisers, ~$50M–$1B AUM) who feel acute pain around documentation consistency, supervision evidence, follow-up tracking, and exam readiness.

**Goal + time box:**
- **Weeks 1–6:** 5 design partners (each provides 2–3 real meeting recordings; iterate weekly)
- **Weeks 7–8:** Convert 3 design partners to paying customers

**Stage:** Pre-revenue; working product with live demo capability

**Offer:**
- *Design partner pilot:* $0–$299 for 30 days (or free with feedback + permission for anonymized screenshots/results). Includes 10–30 meetings processed, export packs, flags, audit trail, retention controls.
- *Paid (post-pilot):* Solo $149–$299/mo · Team $499–$1,499/mo
- *Pricing anchor:* "One exam scramble costs more than a year of Comply Vault."

**30-day customer success definition:**
The CCO/ops owner has processed ≥10 real meetings through Comply Vault, exported at least one exam-ready evidence pack, and confirmed ≥80% reduction in documentation time vs. their prior workflow. They would show the output to an examiner with confidence.

**Constraints:**
- Solo founder; estimate available time for outreach + calls
- Channels: warm intros (LinkedIn, compliance communities), cold email/LinkedIn DM, compliance conferences/webinars
- Compliance/brand constraints: no deceptive claims about AI accuracy; always position as human-review-required; no handling of real client PII in demos without proper controls

**Assumptions & unknowns:**
- *Assumption:* Small RIA CCOs make buying decisions quickly (1–2 week cycle) because they own budget and feel pain daily
- *Assumption:* Compliance consultants will be motivated by per-client leverage (one tool → many clients)
- *Unknown:* Willingness to upload real meeting recordings to a new vendor (security/privacy bar)
- *Unknown:* Whether "free pilot" or "$299 pilot" generates better-quality partners (skin in the game vs. friction)
- *Unknown:* How many CCOs are already using a competing tool vs. truly manual workflows

**Validation plan (next 2 weeks):**
- Run 8–12 discovery calls to validate: (a) current documentation workflow, (b) willingness to try AI-assisted tooling, (c) security/upload concerns, (d) pricing sensitivity
- Test both "free with feedback" and "$299 pilot" positioning to see which attracts serious partners
- Ask every prospect: "What would you need to see to show this to your examiner?"
