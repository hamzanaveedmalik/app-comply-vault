## E) Follow-ups

### Follow-up #1 (2–3 days after initial outreach)

**Subject:** Re: Quick question about meeting documentation at [Firm]

Hi [Name] — quick bump. Is post-meeting documentation and exam readiness on your radar this quarter, or is it a non-issue at [Firm]?

Either way, happy to hear — just trying to learn where the pain is real vs. theoretical.

— [Your Name]

---

### Follow-up #2 (5–7 days — value nugget)

**Subject:** What we're hearing from CCOs at small RIAs

Hi [Name] — one thing we keep hearing from CCOs at firms your size: the problem isn't writing notes — it's that the notes aren't *linked* to supervision evidence in a way an examiner can follow. The chain breaks between "meeting happened" and "here's proof we acted on it."

Does that match what you're seeing at [Firm]?

— [Your Name]

---

### Follow-up #3 (10–14 days — breakup)

**Subject:** Closing the loop

Hi [Name] — no worries if now isn't the right time. If exam readiness or meeting documentation becomes urgent later, happy to reconnect.

In the meantime, would it be useful if I sent a 1-page checklist for "exam-ready meeting supervision documentation"? No strings.

— [Your Name]

## Outreach cadence summary

| Day | Action | Channel |
|---|---|---|
| Day 0 | Initial outreach (warm intro ask OR cold email/DM) | Email or LinkedIn |
| Day 2–3 | Follow-up #1 (bump) | Same channel |
| Day 5–7 | Follow-up #2 (value nugget) | Same channel or alternate |
| Day 10–14 | Follow-up #3 (breakup / offer resource) | Same channel |
| Day 14+ | If no response → move to next target; revisit in 6–8 weeks | — |

**Volume target (Week 1–2):** Send outreach to 15–25 prospects. Aim for 8–12 diagnostic conversations.
