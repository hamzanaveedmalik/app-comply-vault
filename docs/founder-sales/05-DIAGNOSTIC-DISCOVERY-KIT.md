# 5) Diagnostic Discovery Kit (No Demo First)

---

## Call Agenda (20–30 minutes)

| Block | Time | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Context + purpose | 2 min | Set the frame: diagnostic, not a demo |
| 2. Their current workflow + pain | 10–15 min | Understand how they document meetings today |
| 3. Impact + "why now" trigger | 5–8 min | Quantify the cost of the current approach |
| 4. What a good outcome looks like | 3–5 min | Define their success criteria |
| 5. Next steps | 2–3 min | Concrete next action (not "let's circle back") |

---

## Opener (laptop closed)

> "Thanks for making time. I've been talking to CCOs at small RIAs and we keep hearing the same thing: post-meeting documentation is a huge time sink, and when an exam comes up, it's a scramble to reconstruct what happened. I'm not here to demo anything — I mostly want to understand how you handle meeting documentation and supervision evidence today, and whether the pain is real or if I'm hearing a biased sample. Does that sound okay?"

---

## Question Bank

### Current workflow (start here — let them talk)

1. **"Walk me through what happens after a client or adviser meeting."** (Who writes notes? Where do they go? How long does it take?)
2. **"How do you ensure documentation is consistent across advisers?"** (Do all advisers follow the same process?)
3. **"Where does the documentation live?"** (Word docs, Google Docs, CRM, email, compliance platform?)
4. **"How do you link a meeting to a supervision action?"** (If a flag comes up in a meeting, how do you track follow-up and prove you acted on it?)
5. **"What does your current exam prep process look like?"** (If you got a notice tomorrow, how long to pull together supervision evidence for the last 6 months?)

### Pain + impact (quantify)

6. **"How much time per week do you or your team spend on post-meeting documentation?"** (Hours? Per person?)
7. **"What's the cost when documentation falls through the cracks?"** (Has it happened? What was the consequence?)
8. **"Have you had an exam finding related to supervision documentation?"** (Or have you heard of peer firms getting findings?)
9. **"What happens if you do nothing for another 12 months?"** (More advisers, more meetings, same process?)

### Trigger + decision

10. **"What kicked this off — why is this a priority now (or is it)?"** (Exam coming? New hire? Growth? Incident?)
11. **"What have you tried already?"** (Tools, templates, processes? Why didn't they work?)
12. **"Who else would need to be involved if you changed your documentation workflow?"** (Just you? Principal? IT? Outside consultant?)
13. **"What's your timeline?"** (Is this urgent or exploratory?)

### Success criteria

14. **"If you could wave a magic wand, what would your documentation workflow look like?"**
15. **"What would you need to see to feel confident showing AI-assisted documentation to an examiner?"**
16. **"What's your bar for 'this is working'?"** (Time saved? Consistency? Exam confidence?)

---

## How to handle "Can you show me a demo?"

> Don't refuse — redirect: "Absolutely — I'd love to. Let me ask two more questions so I can tailor what I show you to your actual workflow. [Ask questions 1 and 5]. Now let me show you exactly what this would look like for a meeting like the ones you just described."

> Then show a **short, targeted walkthrough** (5 minutes max) using a sample meeting that mirrors their workflow. Not a feature tour — show the *output* they care about: the exam-ready pack.

---

## Next Step Close (choose one based on the conversation)

**If strong pain + interest:**
> "It sounds like this is eating real time and creating real risk. Would it be worth running 2–3 of your actual meetings through the tool to see if the output meets your bar? I can set that up this week."

**If interested but cautious (security/trust concerns):**
> "Totally understand the caution. Would it help if I sent you a sample output from a mock meeting — so you can see exactly what the documentation looks like — before we discuss using real recordings?"

**If mild interest / exploratory:**
> "Let me send you two things: (1) a 1-page decision guide comparing your current approach vs. a pilot, and (2) a sample exam-ready export pack. If it looks relevant, we can set up a short pilot. If not, no worries."

**If not a fit:**
> "Really appreciate your time. It sounds like your current process is working for you. If that changes — especially around exam time — I'd love to reconnect. Can I send you a quick checklist for exam-ready meeting documentation? No strings."
