# 6) Decision Enablement Kit (Beat "No Decision")

---

> Your #1 competitor is not another product — it's "no decision." CCOs know the current process is painful, but changing feels risky. Your job is to make the path forward *safer and easier* than doing nothing.

## A) Decision Guide (1 page — send after discovery call)

### The Decision

Should [Firm Name] change how they handle post-meeting supervision documentation?

### Options

| | Option 1: Do Nothing | Option 2: DIY Improvement | Option 3: Pilot Comply Vault |
|---|---|---|---|
| **What it means** | Keep current Word/Google Docs + spreadsheet workflow | Build internal templates, standardize process, train advisers | 30-day pilot: run 10–30 real meetings through Comply Vault |
| **Time to implement** | 0 (already in place) | 4–8 weeks of CCO time to design + enforce | <1 week to set up; results in first session |
| **Documentation consistency** | Varies by adviser; no enforcement | Improves with training; degrades over time | Automated: same output structure every time |
| **Evidence linking** | Manual; often missing | Manual; depends on discipline | Automatic: transcript → flags → follow-ups → export |
| **Exam readiness** | Scramble when notice arrives | Better, but still manual assembly | Export-ready pack in minutes |
| **Ongoing CCO time** | 3–8 hrs/week on documentation | 2–5 hrs/week (some improvement) | <1 hr/week (review + sign-off) |
| **Risk** | Exam findings; gaps accumulate with growth | High effort; hard to maintain across advisers | New vendor; need to trust AI + data handling |
| **Cost** | $0 (but hidden cost in CCO time + exam risk) | $0 (but CCO time is not free) | $0–$299 for pilot; $149–$299/mo after |

### Recommendation

If documentation burden is real and growing — and especially if an exam is coming or the firm is adding advisers — **Option 3 (pilot)** gives you a definitive answer in 30 days with minimal risk. You'll know within the first 2–3 meetings whether the output meets your examiner-readiness bar.

**The real cost of "do nothing":** One exam scramble — pulling together 6 months of supervision evidence from scattered docs — costs more in CCO time and stress than a year of Comply Vault. And the gap only widens as the firm grows.

## B) Risk Reversal / Pilot Framing

### Pilot: "See It Work Before You Decide"

| Element | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Pilot scope** | Process 10–30 real meeting recordings through Comply Vault over 30 days |
| **Timeline** | 30 days from kickoff |
| **Success criteria (measurable)** | ≥80% reduction in documentation time per meeting · Exam-ready export pack that CCO would confidently show to an examiner · All flags and follow-ups captured and linked |
| **What we do (white glove)** | Set up your workspace · Walk you through first 3 meetings together · Weekly check-in · Answer every question same-day · Help you produce your first exam-ready export pack |
| **What you do** | Provide 2–3 real meeting recordings per week · Review AI-generated output and give honest feedback · 20 minutes/week for check-in calls |
| **Price** | $0 with feedback + permission to use anonymized results, **or** $299 for 30 days (credited toward subscription if you convert) |
| **Data handling** | Recordings processed securely · Access controls + retention rules · You own your data · We can provide a DPA if needed |
| **Exit criteria** | If it's not working by Day 14: we discuss honestly and you walk away. No contracts, no lock-in, no hard feelings. |

### Why this is low-risk for you

- **Bounded:** 30 days, 10–30 meetings. Not a platform migration.
- **Reversible:** If it doesn't meet your bar, stop. Your current process is still there.
- **Measurable:** You'll know in the first 2–3 meetings whether the output quality is sufficient.
- **Human control:** You review and sign off on everything. AI assists — you decide.
- **No procurement:** No contracts, no legal review required for a pilot at this price point.

## Objection Handling Guide

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| **"I need to think about it."** | "Totally fair. What specific questions would help you decide? I can send you [decision guide / sample output / reference] to make it easier." |
| **"I'm not comfortable uploading real meeting recordings."** | "I hear that a lot — and it's the right concern. Let me walk you through our data handling: [access controls, retention, encryption, DPA]. We can also start with a sample recording so you see the output before using real ones." |
| **"What if the AI gets something wrong?"** | "It will — that's why human review is built in. You sign off on every output before it goes into your supervision file. The AI does the labor; you keep the judgment. We've designed it this way specifically because compliance documentation can't be auto-generated without review." |
| **"We're too small / this is overkill."** | "Actually, small firms feel this pain the most — you don't have a team to split the work. And you face the same SEC examination standards as larger firms. The pilot is designed for firms exactly your size." |
| **"I want to wait until our next exam."** | "I understand. One thought: the firms that feel the most pain during exams are the ones that didn't have the documentation *before* the notice arrived. A 30-day pilot now means you'd have 6+ months of exam-ready documentation by the time an exam comes." |
| **"What about [competitor / existing tool]?"** | "Great question — what are you using today and what's working vs. not? [Listen.] The gap we typically hear about is that existing tools handle communications archiving, not meeting-level supervision documentation with evidence linking. But let me understand your specific setup." |
| **"I need to run this by [principal / board]."** | "Of course. Would it help if I sent a 1-page summary of the pilot (scope, cost, success criteria, exit clause) that you can forward? And would it be useful to include them in the next call?" |
