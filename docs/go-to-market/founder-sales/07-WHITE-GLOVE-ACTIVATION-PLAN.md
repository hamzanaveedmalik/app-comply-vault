# 7) White-Glove Activation Plan (Prevent "Yes → Silence")

---

> A deal is not "won" until the customer is **activated** — implemented, using the product on real meetings, and getting value. The most common early-stage failure mode is "yes → silence": the CCO agrees to pilot, then goes quiet. White-glove onboarding prevents this.

---

## Activation Definition

**Activated when:**
1. Workspace is set up with the CCO's access controls and retention preferences
2. ≥3 real meeting recordings have been processed
3. CCO has reviewed and signed off on at least one output
4. First exam-ready export pack has been generated
5. CCO confirms: "I would show this to an examiner"

**Time-to-first-value target:** ≤3 days from kickoff (first meeting processed and reviewed)

---

## Implementation Checklist

### Pre-kickoff (before or during close)

- [ ] Kickoff call scheduled on the calendar *before the pilot agreement is finalized*
- [ ] CCO has clear expectations: what they provide (recordings), what they get (outputs), timeline
- [ ] Data handling questions answered (security, retention, access controls, DPA if needed)
- [ ] First 2–3 meeting recordings identified (CCO knows which meetings they'll upload first)

### Day 0: Kickoff call (30 minutes)

- [ ] Workspace created and configured (access controls, retention rules)
- [ ] Walk through the product together: upload → processing → review → sign-off → export
- [ ] Process the first meeting recording *live on the call* (CCO watches it happen)
- [ ] CCO reviews the output: transcript, flags, follow-ups, evidence links
- [ ] Capture immediate feedback: What's accurate? What's missing? What format would you change?
- [ ] Set weekly check-in cadence (same day/time each week for 4 weeks)

### Day 1–3: First value

- [ ] CCO uploads 1–2 more recordings independently
- [ ] You process and deliver outputs within 24 hours (or real-time if product supports it)
- [ ] CCO reviews and signs off
- [ ] Confirm: "Is this output quality sufficient for your supervision file?"
- [ ] Generate first exam-ready export pack from the processed meetings

### Day 4–7: Habit formation

- [ ] CCO has a routine: upload meeting → review output → sign off
- [ ] Any issues or friction points identified and resolved
- [ ] 5+ meetings processed total
- [ ] Time comparison documented: old workflow vs. Comply Vault

---

## 30-Day Success Plan

### Week 1: Setup + First Value

**Goal:** CCO processes 3+ meetings and confirms output quality

| Action | Owner | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Kickoff call + first live processing | Both | Meeting recording processed; output reviewed |
| 2 more recordings uploaded + processed | CCO uploads; CV processes | 3 outputs delivered |
| First export pack generated | Both | Exam-ready pack exported |
| Feedback captured (accuracy, format, gaps) | You | Feedback log updated |

**Week 1 check-in agenda (15 min):**
- How's the output quality? What would you change?
- Any friction in the upload/review process?
- Time comparison: how long did this take vs. your old process?
- Plan for next week's recordings

---

### Week 2: Expand + Iterate

**Goal:** 10+ meetings processed; output quality refined based on feedback

| Action | Owner | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Process 5–7 more meetings | CCO uploads; CV processes | 10+ meetings total |
| Iterate on output based on Week 1 feedback | You | Improved outputs |
| Mid-pilot review: go/no-go checkpoint | Both | Decision documented |
| Begin capturing proof points (time saved, quality quotes) | You | Proof points log |

**Week 2 check-in agenda (20 min — mid-pilot review):**
- Are outputs meeting your examiner-readiness bar?
- Go/no-go: should we continue the pilot?
- What would make this a "must-have" vs. "nice-to-have"?
- Any new use cases emerging (e.g., adviser supervision reviews)?

---

### Week 3: Deepen + Prove

**Goal:** 15–20 meetings processed; proof points captured; expansion explored

| Action | Owner | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Process 5–10 more meetings | CCO uploads; CV processes | 15–20 meetings total |
| Capture proof points: time saved (%), quality improvement, CCO quotes | You | Proof points for case study |
| Explore: additional users? Additional workflows? | Both | Expansion notes |
| Discuss pricing / subscription if converting | Both | Pricing conversation started |

**Week 3 check-in agenda (15 min):**
- Results so far: time saved, documentation quality, exam readiness confidence
- Would you add another user (adviser, associate)?
- What's missing before you'd subscribe?

---

### Week 4: Decide + Transition

**Goal:** Pilot concludes; decision on paid subscription

| Action | Owner | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Final batch of meetings processed | CCO uploads; CV processes | 20–30 meetings total |
| Generate comprehensive exam-ready export pack | Both | Full export pack |
| Pilot results summary (time saved, meetings processed, quality score) | You | 1-page results summary |
| Decision call: subscribe, extend, or exit | Both | Decision documented |

**Week 4 check-in agenda (30 min — decision call):**
- Review pilot results summary together
- Decision: subscribe ($149–$299/mo solo) or exit?
- If subscribing: set up billing, confirm ongoing cadence, discuss expansion
- If not: understand why; capture learnings; leave door open
- Ask: "Would you be willing to be a reference or share anonymized results?"

---

## Follow-Up Cadence (Preventing Silence)

| Timing | Action | Channel |
|---|---|---|
| Day 0 | Kickoff call | Video call |
| Day 1 | "How did the first independent upload go?" | Email / Slack |
| Day 3 | "First export pack is ready — take a look" | Email |
| Day 7 | Week 1 check-in | Video call |
| Day 10 | Async: "Any recordings from this week?" (gentle nudge) | Email / DM |
| Day 14 | Week 2 check-in (mid-pilot review + go/no-go) | Video call |
| Day 21 | Week 3 check-in | Video call |
| Day 28–30 | Week 4 decision call | Video call |

### If the CCO goes quiet (no uploads after Day 3)

**Day 5 nudge:**
> "Hi [Name] — just checking in. I noticed we haven't had new recordings since kickoff. Is there anything blocking you (time, security concern, upload issue)? Happy to hop on a 10-minute call to sort it out."

**Day 8 (if still quiet):**
> "Hi [Name] — I want to make sure we get value out of the pilot time. Would it help if I processed a recording on your behalf? Just send me a file and I'll deliver the output — you can review without lifting a finger."

**Day 12 (final check before mid-pilot):**
> "Hi [Name] — our mid-pilot check-in is in 2 days. If you can upload 1–2 more recordings before then, we'll have enough data to make a real assessment. If the timing doesn't work, no worries — we can extend or pause."

---

## Escalation Path (If Blocked)

| Blocker | Escalation |
|---|---|
| **Technical issue** (upload fails, processing error) | You fix it same-day; follow up with confirmation |
| **Security/data concern** | Provide documentation (encryption, access controls, retention, DPA); offer call with CCO to walk through |
| **CCO too busy to upload** | Offer to process recordings on their behalf; reduce ask to 1 recording |
| **Quality concern** (output not accurate enough) | Schedule 30-min review; iterate on output; adjust extraction/flagging |
| **Lost interest / deprioritized** | Honest conversation: "Is this still a priority? If not, no hard feelings — but I'd love to understand what changed." |
