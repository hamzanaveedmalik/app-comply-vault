# 9) Risks / Open Questions / Next Steps

---

## Risks

| Risk | Severity | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| **CCOs won't upload real recordings to a new vendor** | High | Lead with security posture (access controls, retention, encryption, DPA). Offer sample/mock meeting first. Address on the first call — don't wait for it to stall the pilot. |
| **AI output quality not sufficient for examiner scrutiny** | High | Position as human-review-required. Build obvious review/sign-off step. Iterate output quality aggressively during pilot. Set expectation: "AI drafts, you approve." |
| **"No decision" — CCOs acknowledge pain but don't act** | High | Decision enablement kit (Section 6). Make "do nothing" cost explicit. Bounded pilot with clear exit. MAP with dates and owners. |
| **Design partners go quiet after agreeing to pilot** | Medium | White-glove activation (Section 7). Schedule kickoff before agreement. Follow-up cadence. Proactive nudges. Process first meeting live on kickoff call. |
| **Pricing too high for small firms / too low to signal value** | Medium | Test both $0 and $299 pilot pricing. Anchor to exam scramble cost. If $299 causes friction, default to free-with-feedback for design partners. |
| **Compliance consultants want custom integration / multi-tenant** | Medium | Defer for now. Validate demand with 1–2 consultants. If real, scope "consultant mode" as a post-launch add-on. Don't build it before validation. |
| **Regulatory sensitivity — AI in compliance raises skepticism** | Medium | Lead with "human sign-off" positioning. Never claim AI replaces CCO judgment. Show audit trail and version history. Reference healthcare compliance background. |
| **Solo founder bandwidth — can't handle 5+ active pilots simultaneously** | Medium | Stagger pilot starts. Automate what you can (processing, export). Limit active pilots to 3–5 at a time. Focus on quality over quantity. |
| **Competitor enters market (RegTech, existing compliance platforms)** | Low (short-term) | Speed advantage: you're focused on this exact wedge. Larger platforms move slowly. Win design partners now; build switching costs through relationship + data. |

## Open Questions

### Product / market

1. **What's the CCO's actual documentation workflow today?** (Validate during discovery — do they really use Word/spreadsheets, or is there more tooling than assumed?)
2. **Is "exam readiness" the right primary pain, or is "daily documentation time" more motivating?** (Listen for which framing gets the stronger reaction)
3. **How do CCOs currently handle meeting recordings?** (Do they even record meetings today? If not, Comply Vault requires a behavior change *before* the product can add value)
4. **What's the minimum output quality that a CCO would trust?** (Is 90% accurate enough? 95%? What errors are unacceptable?)
5. **Do compliance consultants buy tools, or do they expect their clients to buy?** (This affects go-to-market for the secondary ICP)

### Sales process

6. **Is a 30-day pilot the right length, or can you validate in 14 days?** (Shorter = faster learning cycle, less bandwidth per partner)
7. **Does "free with feedback" attract tire-kickers, or does $299 create friction that kills momentum?** (Test both in first 2 weeks)
8. **What's the actual decision cycle at a 5-person RIA?** (Can the CCO/principal decide in one call, or is there always a second conversation?)

### Data / security

9. **What data handling assurances do CCOs need before uploading recordings?** (SOC 2? DPA? Just a verbal walkthrough? Varies by firm?)
10. **Are there RIA firms where recording client meetings is prohibited or requires client consent?** (If so, this limits the addressable market for those firms — but internal adviser meetings may still be fair game)

## Next Steps (Executable — Week 1)

### Immediate (Days 1–3)

- [ ] **Build target list:** Use IAPD + LinkedIn to identify 25 small RIAs matching primary ICP criteria. Note CCO names, firm size, AUM, and any trigger signals.
- [ ] **Check warm paths:** For each top-25 target, check LinkedIn for mutual connections. Draft warm intro asks for the strongest 5–8 warm paths.
- [ ] **Prepare demo environment:** Ensure you can run a live demo (upload a sample meeting → processing → output → export pack) reliably in <10 minutes. Practice the flow.
- [ ] **Draft first outreach batch:** Personalize cold emails (Section 4B) for the first 10–15 targets. Personalize the "[trigger/pain]" and "[company]" fields.

### Week 1 (Days 1–7)

- [ ] **Send first outreach batch:** 15–25 messages (mix of warm intro asks + cold emails/DMs).
- [ ] **Engage in 1–2 compliance communities:** Join relevant Slack groups, LinkedIn groups, or forums. Contribute value (not pitch). Identify prospects organically.
- [ ] **Schedule first discovery calls:** Aim for 3–5 calls booked by end of Week 1.
- [ ] **Set up pipeline tracker:** Use the tracker from Section 8. Update after every interaction.

### Week 2 (Days 8–14)

- [ ] **Run 5–8 discovery calls.** Use the diagnostic discovery kit (Section 5). Capture call notes with verbatim language.
- [ ] **Propose first pilots.** For prospects with strong pain, share the decision guide (Section 6A) and pilot terms (Section 6B).
- [ ] **Iterate messaging.** Based on discovery call language, refine outreach copy for the next batch.
- [ ] **First weekly review.** Use the template from Section 8. Adjust ICP, messaging, and outreach based on what you've learned.

### Week 3–4

- [ ] **First 2–3 pilots kick off.** White-glove onboard each partner (Section 7).
- [ ] **Continue outreach.** Fill pipeline to reach 5 design partners by Week 6.
- [ ] **Mid-pilot reviews.** Go/no-go checkpoint at Day 14 of each pilot.

### Week 5–8

- [ ] **Reach 5 active design partners.** Iterate product and onboarding based on feedback.
- [ ] **Begin conversion conversations.** Use MAP (Section 6C) and pilot results to drive decisions.
- [ ] **Close 3 paying customers by Week 8.**
- [ ] **Capture proof points.** Time-saved metrics, CCO quotes, anonymized results for future case studies.

## Assumptions & Unknowns Summary

| Assumption | Confidence | Validation method |
|---|---|---|
| Small RIA CCOs feel acute documentation pain | Medium-High | Discovery calls (first 5–8) |
| They'll upload real recordings to a new vendor | Medium | Ask directly in discovery; test in pilot |
| "Free with feedback" vs. $299 — which works better | Low | Test both in Weeks 1–2 |
| 80% doc time reduction is achievable | Medium | Measure in first 3 pilot meetings |
| CCOs are the decision maker (no procurement) | Medium-High | Confirm in discovery calls |
| Compliance consultants want leverage tooling | Medium | Test with 1–2 consultant conversations |
| Single exam scramble cost > 1 year of Comply Vault | Medium | Validate cost data in discovery |
