ComplyVault — Security Overview
Version: 2026-07-26
================================================

This document describes security and compliance-relevant controls that are
implemented in the ComplyVault product today. It is provided for download
without an email gate (CV-WEB-01).

Certification
-------------
SOC 2 Type II: Not started.
No observation window has been opened and no SOC 2 report has been issued.

Implemented controls (summary)
------------------------------
- Sealed audit packs written to Object Lock storage, keyed by SHA-256, with
  fiscal-year retention expiry.
- Append-oriented seal ledger (RecordSeal) with SELECT/INSERT-oriented app
  privileges by design.
- Fiscal-year retention policy on the workspace (years + fiscal year end).
- Documented source-media posture (RETAIN vs DISCARD) required before ingest.
- Supersession of sealed records without deleting the original seal.
- External deposits labeled as convenience copies; sealed object is system
  of record.
- Encrypted integration secrets at rest; workspace RBAC; audit events on
  compliance mutations.

Not claimed here
----------------
- Legal hold as a completed marketed control (pending CV-TR-05).
- SOC 2 "in progress" without a published observation-window start date.
- FCA / UK supervision product track (removed per CV-WEB-04).

Contact
